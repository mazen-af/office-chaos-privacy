01Overview

Office Chaos is a simple arcade game: hold to rise, release to fall, and dodge your way through the office. This policy explains what little data the app touches, why, and who it passes through — Google's ad and backend services, and nothing else. There's no account system, no email collection, and no access to your contacts, photos, or files.

02Information we collect

The app collects two narrow categories of information, both tied to gameplay rather than to you personally.

Leaderboard entries

If you choose to save a score, you're asked to enter a nickname (up to 14 characters, chosen by you — a real name isn't required or requested). That nickname, your score, and a server timestamp are stored in the game's Firebase/Firestore database and shown publicly on the in-game leaderboard. Your nickname is also saved on your device so you aren't asked for it again next time you play.

Advertising data

Office Chaos shows optional rewarded ads (to continue a run after a game over) and occasional interstitial ads, served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its ad partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, general (non-precise) location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads. This happens through Google's SDK, not code we write — see the next section for what Google does with it.

03Third-party services

Two Google services do the heavy lifting behind the scenes:

  • Google AdMob — serves the rewarded and interstitial ads described above. Google's own policies govern what it does with advertising data: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — stores leaderboard entries (nickname, score, timestamp). Covered under Firebase's privacy and security policy.

04How we use information

Leaderboard data is used only to show rankings inside the app. Advertising data is used by Google and its partners to select and measure the ads shown to you. We don't sell data, and we don't use it for anything beyond running the game and keeping ads relevant.

05Your choices

  • Skip the leaderboard. Saving a score is optional — you can close the "Your name" prompt and keep playing without ever submitting one.
  • Pick a nickname carefully. Whatever you type is shown publicly, so avoid using your real name or anything identifying.
  • Limit ad personalization. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Skip ads entirely. Ads are optional — the "watch ad" continue is only offered after a game over, and declining it doesn't block normal play.

06Data retention & security

Leaderboard entries are kept in Firestore to keep the leaderboard meaningful over time; there's currently no self-service way to delete an individual entry, so please don't submit anything you wouldn't want to stay public — contact us below if you'd like an entry removed. Firestore's access rules only allow new entries to be created, not edited or deleted, which also means a submitted score can't be altered later. As with any online service, no method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we don't retain anything beyond the nickname/score/timestamp described above.

07Children's privacy

Office Chaos is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through the leaderboard, contact us below and we'll remove it.
